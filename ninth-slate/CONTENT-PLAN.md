# Ninth Slate — Organic Content Plan

No paid promotion required.

## Objective

Build credibility slowly through proof of engineering work.

## Content pillars

### 1. Build notes
Short explanations of a real engineering problem and how it was approached.

### 2. Product thinking
Why a feature was designed a certain way, without leaking unreleased product details.

### 3. QA and debugging
Before/after examples, regression thinking, responsive fixes and reliability work.

### 4. AI without hype
Practical AI workflows, evaluation, context, privacy and cost tradeoffs.

### 5. Security-minded engineering
Access control, RLS, auth flows and safe engineering practices.

### 6. Interactive systems
Performance, persistence, world systems and simulation engineering.

## Cadence

Foundation phase:
1 strong post per week is enough.

Do not post daily filler.

## First 6 posts

1. Introducing Ninth Slate
2. Why we separate experiments from production
3. How we approach bug fixing in existing codebases
4. Why AI features need evaluation, privacy and cost controls
5. What "security-minded development" means in practice
6. Building interactive systems without sacrificing maintainability
