# Ninth Slate — Brand Foundation

Status: Working brand approved for internal/product organization. No legal trademark registration or paid domain purchase is implied.

## Core identity

**Name:** Ninth Slate  
**Descriptor:** Independent Technology Studio  
**Primary line:** Software · AI · Security · Interactive

## Meaning

"Slate" represents a clean surface on which new systems, products and ideas can be built. "Ninth" gives the brand a distinctive identity and supports a narrative of completion, evolution and the next stage.

## Positioning

Ninth Slate is an independent technology studio focused on designing, building and evolving software, AI systems, security-focused solutions and interactive digital products.

The studio is intended to separate Nicola Rigoni's personal freelance identity from independent product development.

- **Nicola Rigoni** = Founder / Full-Stack Developer / freelance professional
- **Ninth Slate** = product and technology brand
- **Client work** = may be delivered by Nicola directly or later through Ninth Slate
- **Private products** = branded under Ninth Slate, not exposed as a list of personal projects

## Divisions

### Ninth Slate Software
Web applications, mobile applications, SaaS, internal tools, business software and integrations.

### Ninth Slate AI
AI-powered products, assistants, agents, RAG systems, automation and intelligent workflows.

### Ninth Slate Security
Security-minded engineering, application security, cybersecurity tooling and research.

### Ninth Slate Interactive
Games, simulations and interactive digital experiences.

### Ninth Slate Labs
Experimental products, prototypes, research and emerging technologies.

## Voice

- Direct
- Technical but understandable
- Product-focused
- No hype
- No fake scale
- No "revolutionary", "industry-leading", or unsupported claims
- Prefer concrete capabilities and verifiable results

## Visual direction

- Premium / minimal
- Dark graphite / near-black base
- Electric-blue accent
- Strong geometric monogram
- Wide uppercase wordmark
- High contrast
- No gradients unless subtle and functional
- Avoid generic AI imagery, glowing brains, circuit clichés and stock-tech backgrounds
