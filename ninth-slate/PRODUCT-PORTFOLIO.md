# Ninth Slate — Product Portfolio Architecture

## Rule

Ninth Slate owns the product identity. Nicola Rigoni remains founder and developer, but products intended for the studio should not be presented publicly as personal side projects.

## Portfolio map

| Product | Public attribution | Division | Public naming policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Academy Learning | A Ninth Slate product | Ninth Slate Software / AI | Product name may stay Academy Learning; founder name is secondary |
| Remember | A Ninth Slate product | Ninth Slate Software | Product name may stay Remember; personal attribution removed from marketing |
| Fitness AI App | A Ninth Slate product | Ninth Slate AI / Software | Final commercial product name still to be decided |
| Valleverde | By Ninth Slate Interactive | Ninth Slate Interactive | Game identity remains separate from founder identity |
| Business Management System | Private client/internal engineering | Ninth Slate Software | Do not expose sensitive client/business details |
| I Rapaci di Eddy | Client work / public case study | Client work | Credit Nicola/Ninth Slate only where appropriate |

## Attribution hierarchy

1. Product brand
2. Ninth Slate
3. Division, when useful
4. Founder attribution only on company/about/team pages

Examples:

**Academy Learning**  
A Ninth Slate product

## Confidentiality

Do not publicly reveal:
- private repository names when unnecessary
- internal client logic
- operational data
- unreleased commercial mechanics
- credentials, infrastructure secrets or private URLs

## Founder separation

Nicola's freelance profiles should sell skills and services.
Ninth Slate channels should sell studio capability and products.
Product pages should sell the product itself.
