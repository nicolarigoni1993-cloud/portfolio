# Ninth Slate — Brand Tokens

## Color direction

Primary background: near-black / graphite  
Primary text: off-white  
Secondary text: cool gray  
Accent: electric blue

Suggested digital values:
- Background: #080B10
- Surface: #0F141D
- Text: #F3F6FB
- Muted: #939BAA
- Border: #202835
- Accent: #1769FF

These are working digital tokens, not a legal or print color specification.

## Typography direction

Use a modern geometric sans-serif with:
- strong uppercase wordmark
- wide tracking for labels
- compact, readable body text
- no decorative sci-fi font for paragraphs

Until a final licensed/free font is selected, system sans-serif is preferred over embedding random external font files.

## Logo usage

Primary:
- symbol + NINTH SLATE wordmark

Secondary:
- symbol only for avatar/favicon

Do:
- preserve clear space
- use high contrast
- keep the mark simple

Do not:
- stretch the logo
- add shadows/glow for normal corporate use
- place it over noisy imagery
- combine multiple slogans inside the mark
