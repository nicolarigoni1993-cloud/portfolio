# Ninth Slate — Public FAQ Draft

## What is Ninth Slate?
Ninth Slate is an independent technology studio focused on software, AI systems, security-focused engineering and interactive digital products.

## Is Ninth Slate a large company?
No. Ninth Slate is currently being established as an independent studio and product brand. Public communication should remain accurate about its current scale.

## Who founded Ninth Slate?
Ninth Slate was founded by Nicola Rigoni, a Full-Stack Developer and Computer Engineering student.

## What does Ninth Slate build?
The studio works across web and mobile software, backend systems, intelligent workflows, security-minded solutions, automation, games and simulations.

## Does Ninth Slate accept client work?
Yes, selectively. During the foundation phase, client enquiries are handled directly by founder Nicola Rigoni.

## Are all Ninth Slate products public?
No. Several products are in private development and are only published when they are ready for public association with the brand.

## Is Ninth Slate an AI company?
AI is one of the studio's focus areas, but Ninth Slate is broader: Software, AI, Security and Interactive.

## Where is Ninth Slate based?
The founder is based in Italy. The studio is designed around remote-first digital work.

## Is Ninth Slate already a legally incorporated company?
Public wording should not imply a legal corporate form until one actually exists. "Independent Technology Studio" and "product brand" are the preferred descriptions during the foundation phase.
